API Requester - Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 20, 2026

At API Requester ("we", "us", or "our"), we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application, API Requester, handles your data, specifically focusing on how we process and use the information related to your API requests.

1. Information We Access

API Requester does not collect or store any personal data automatically from your device. The application solely interacts with data that you manually provide, such as URLs, headers, request bodies, and history, to facilitate your API interactions.

2. How We Use the Data

The data you enter into API Requester is used exclusively for the purpose of executing HTTP requests, saving your request history, and managing your bookmarks. This data is stored locally on your device to ensure a performant and seamless experience. API Requester does not transmit, share, or store your requests or any data associated with them to any of our servers or external services. Data is only transmitted to the specific endpoints and URLs that you explicitly instruct the app to connect to.

3. Data Collection, Sharing, and Disclosure

API Requester does not collect, store, or share any personal data or usage analytics with us. All information related to your API usage is processed locally on your device and is not transmitted to any external servers or third-party tracking services.

4. Data Storage and Security

Since API Requester operates entirely on your device, all your request data, headers, and payload information remain secure within your local environment. The app relies on Apple's built-in security features to ensure that the data is protected against unauthorized access.

5. Third-Party Services

API Requester does not integrate with or rely on any third-party telemetry or analytics services for its operation. The app functions independently on your device, ensuring that no external entities have access to the requests you generate or the data you inspect.

6. Children's Privacy

API Requester is designed for use by individuals of all ages and does not knowingly collect any personal information from users, including children under the age of 13. Since no analytics data is collected or sent to us, the app complies with child privacy laws by design.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.
