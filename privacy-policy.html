Privacy Policy

API Requester is a tool for talking to your APIs, not for collecting data about you. This policy explains what the app stores, where it goes and what we never see.

1. Who we are

API Requester is developed by Volodymyr Sichka, an independent developer ("we", "us"). If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at volodymyr.sichka@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. API Requester has no sign-up or account, contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and we do not run servers that receive data from the app.

3. Requests you send

When you send a request, the app connects directly from your device to the URL you entered, with the headers, body and credentials you added. We never see these requests or their responses.

The servers you contact receive your request and, as with any network connection, your IP address. Their own privacy policies apply. The built-in examples contact public test services (jsonplaceholder.typicode.com, httpbin.org and picsum.photos) only when you choose to send them.

4. Data stored by the app

To be useful, the app keeps the following on your device:

  • Saved requests: name, method, URL, headers and body.
  • History: the requests you sent, with status code, duration and size. Response bodies are not saved.
  • Environments: their names, colors and variables.
  • Usage statistics: how many requests you sent, by method and status code, shown in Settings.
  • Settings, such as timeout and formatting preferences.

If you are signed in to iCloud, saved requests, history, environments and statistics sync to your other devices through Apple's iCloud key-value storage, under your own Apple Account. We cannot access this data.

Tokens, passwords and API keys you put into headers, auth or variables are stored and synced together with the request they belong to. You can remove them at any time.

The app also keeps a few counters on your device, such as the number of successful requests, to decide when to show Apple's rating prompt. They are not synced or shared.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Data to clear history, delete saved requests or reset statistics, and Settings → Environments to delete environments. Clearing data in the app also removes it from iCloud and your other devices. Deleting the app removes its data from that device, but synced data stays in your iCloud and comes back if you reinstall, so clear it in the app first to remove it everywhere.

5. Purchases

API Requester Pro is sold as an auto-renewable subscription through the App Store. Apple processes the payment, and we do not receive your name, email address or payment details. The app checks your subscription status on your device using Apple's StoreKit.

6. Apple services

iCloud sync, App Store purchases and the in-app rating prompt are provided by Apple and covered by the Apple Privacy Policy. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may provide us with aggregated, anonymous crash and usage reports. They do not identify you.

7. Children's privacy

API Requester is a developer tool and is not directed at children. Because we collect no personal information, we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

8. Your choices and rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on our side. All data is under your control in the app and in your iCloud account. If you have a question or request regarding privacy, contact us and we will respond promptly.

9. Changes to this policy

If our practices change, we will update this page and the date at the top. If a change affects how your data is handled, we will describe it in the app's release notes.

10. Contact

Questions or concerns about privacy: volodymyr.sichka@gmail.com.
