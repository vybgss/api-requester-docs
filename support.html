Getting started
Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 17 or later, Mac with macOS 14 Sonoma or later, and Apple Vision Pro. A Pro subscription works on all of them when you use the same Apple Account.

How do I send my first request?

Type or paste a URL into the address bar and tap Send, or press Return (⌘↩ with a keyboard). Not sure where to start? Tap one of the examples on the Request screen.

If you leave out the scheme, https:// is added for you. You can turn this off in Settings → Add https:// automatically.

Can I import a cURL command?

Yes. Paste a curl … command straight into the address bar, or use Import (⇧⌘I). The method, URL, headers, body and -u credentials are filled in for you.

How do query parameters work?

Edit them as rows in the Params tab or directly in the URL. Both stay in sync, and what you typed is not re-encoded.

How do I add authentication?

Open the Auth tab and choose Bearer token, Basic auth or API key. The helper writes a regular header, so what you see in Headers is exactly what is sent and exported.

Features
How do environments and {{variables}} work?

Open Settings → Environments (⌥⌘E on a keyboard) and create environments such as Production, Staging or Local. Add variables like baseUrl or token to each one.

Use {{baseUrl}} anywhere in the URL, headers or body. The active environment is applied when you send. If a variable isn't defined, it is highlighted and the app tells you before sending.

How do I export a request as code?

Open the ••• menu and choose Export… (⇧⌘E). Pick a format: plain HTTP message, cURL, HTTPie, JavaScript fetch, Python requests or Swift URLSession. You can substitute variables from the active environment, then copy or share the result.

The plain HTTP message is free; cURL and code export are part of Pro.

What do the timing numbers mean?

The Timing tab breaks a request into phases: DNS lookup, TCP connect, TLS handshake, request sent, waiting for the first byte (TTFB) and content download. Below it you'll find the protocol, remote address, TLS version and whether the connection was reused.

A large response is slow to display. What can I do?

Go to Settings → Format responses and choose up to which size JSON is pretty-printed and highlighted (from 20 KB to 5 MB, or Never). Larger responses are shown as raw text, which is always fast.

A request times out or doesn't follow a redirect.

Settings → Timeout sets how long to wait for a response (5 to 300 seconds, 30 by default). Settings → Follow redirects controls whether 3xx responses are followed automatically.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings, tap the plan card at the top, then tap Restore at the bottom of the screen. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account you used to subscribe.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open the Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → API Requester, or go to apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. Pro subscribers can also tap Manage Subscription on the plan screen in the app.

To avoid the next charge, cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Refund requests are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Data and privacy
Do my requests sync between devices?

Yes. Saved requests, history and environments sync through iCloud to every device signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on. Changes sync automatically in the background.

Why does history keep only 10 requests?

The free Core plan keeps the 10 most recent requests. With Pro, history keeps up to 1,000 requests.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Settings → Data and choose Clear history, Delete saved requests or Reset statistics. Environments can be deleted in Settings → Environments. Because the data syncs through iCloud, it is removed from your other devices too.

Does API Requester collect my data?

No. There's no account, no analytics and no ads. Requests go directly from your device to the servers you choose, and we never see them. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
